RF100-VL
========

Source: https://github.com/roboflow/rf100-vl
Commit: 1c1ecd84cf2865270702121311e1d9e5f2a861aa
License: Apache-2.0

The dataset-name-to-domain mapping in va_bench/data/rf100vl_domains.json is
derived from rf100vl/assets/dataset_name_to_category.json at the commit above.
Four labels are normalized to the paper's canonical display names: Lab Imaging
to Medical, Misc to Other, Sport to Sports, and Flora/Fauna to Flora and Fauna.

The version-locked downloader integrates with the public rf100vl package API.
It deliberately addresses project wrappers by dataset name instead of package
index and downloads the exact Roboflow version ids recorded in versions.json.

The Apache License 2.0 is reproduced in the repository's LICENSE file.
